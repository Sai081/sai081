## About me

I’m **Sai Sankar**, an AI Engineering graduate from India. I build and study practical AI systems at the intersection of **LLMs, retrieval, agentic workflows, open-source models, and enterprise integration**.

I enjoy the full learning loop: understanding a model or framework, building a focused prototype, debugging its limits, and refining the idea into something useful.

- 🔭 Currently exploring **LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, and agentic AI**
- 🧠 Deepening my knowledge of **RAG, fine-tuning, PEFT/LoRA, and multi-agent systems**
- 🛠️ Building with **Python, Transformers, vector databases, APIs, and SAP CPI**
- 🏢 Completed an internship at **VKollab Technologies**, gaining hands-on exposure to SAP CPI, enterprise integration, and how startups operate
- 🤝 Open to collaborations, learning opportunities, and thoughtful AI conversations
